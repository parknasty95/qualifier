Grounded benefits assistant

Ask Qualifier a benefits question

Grounded AI mode. Qualifier retrieves only approved Indiana/federal benefit information loaded into the server. The AI can explain your screening results, benefit estimates, and next steps, but it does not make the official eligibility decision.

Try “Why did I get these results?”, “How much SNAP could I receive?”, “What should I gather before applying?”, “What is CHIP?”, or “How does Section 8 work?”

Ask about SNAP, Medicaid, WIC, LIHEAP/EAP, Housing Choice Vouchers, CHIP, school meals, or TANF.
