4 · Citizenship / immigration
Which best describes you for this screening?

Noncitizen SNAP eligibility changed in 2025 and depends on specific legal classifications. This prototype will not infer a classification from nationality or free text.

5 · Income & resources
Countable resources you can identify ($)
Income pattern
Average monthly gross business receipts ($)
Monthly business expenses you believe may be allowable ($)

The tool records these figures but does not automatically decide which business expenses are allowable without a fully encoded verified expense rule. It will tell you exactly what remains to verify.

Grounded benefits assistant

Ask an Indiana SNAP question

Grounded AI mode. When deployed with the included server function, questions are matched to approved Indiana SNAP passages and only those passages (plus your screening trace when relevant) are sent to the AI. If the endpoint is unavailable, the tool automatically falls back to local grounded retrieval.

Ask general Indiana SNAP questions at any time. After screening, the assistant can also explain your specific result. If the approved material does not establish an answer, it will not guess.

Ask me things like “What is a countable resource?”, “Does my car count?”, “Who is in my SNAP household?”, “What deductions are allowed?”, “Can a college student qualify?”, or—after screening—“Why did I get this result?”

Methodology & safety

How the prototype is designed

1 · Ground truthEligibility facts come from approved Indiana FSSA / government materials, not model memory.
2 · Rules engineThresholds and encoded eligibility branches are deterministic calculations.
3 · Grounded assistantThe assistant explains retrieved policy and the screening trace; it does not adjudicate eligibility.
4 · Fail closedMissing, conflicting, or unencoded rules produce review/verification—not a fabricated answer.

Production architecture: questionnaire → structured facts → verified rules engine → result + reason codes → retrieval from approved documents → LLM explanation with citations. The LLM never supplies thresholds or overrides the rules engine.
