Qualifier

Answer one questionnaire to identify Indiana public-benefit programs that may be worth applying for. Qualifier uses program-specific rules and official government sources; it does not make an official eligibility determination.

1 · What are you looking for?
Which type of benefits would you like Qualifier to screen?

Food screens SNAP, WIC, free/reduced-price school meals, and TANF family cash assistance. Healthcare screens Medicaid and CHIP. Housing & utilities screens LIHEAP/EAP and Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8). “All” screens every program.

Grounded benefits assistant

Ask Qualifier a benefits question

Grounded AI mode. Qualifier retrieves only approved Indiana/federal benefit passages loaded into the server. The AI explains that evidence and your deterministic screening trace; it is instructed not to invent thresholds, exceptions, or eligibility conclusions.

Try “What is WIC?”, “How does CHIP work in Indiana?”, “What documents do I need for energy assistance?”, “How does Section 8 eligibility work?”, or after screening, “Why did I get these results?”

Ask about SNAP, Medicaid, WIC, LIHEAP/EAP, Housing Choice Vouchers, CHIP, school meals, or TANF.
Ground-truth corpus

Official sources used by this version

Rules are effective-dated where possible. Qualifier should be revalidated when agencies publish new standards.

SNAP / TANF: Indiana FSSA DFR Program Policy Manual; Indiana SNAP and TANF program pages.
Medicaid / CHIP: Indiana Medicaid Eligibility Guide; Indiana Health Coverage Program Policy Manual; Hoosier Healthwise / Package C materials.
WIC: Indiana Department of Health WIC Eligibility Requirements and 2026 income guidelines.
LIHEAP/EAP: Indiana IHCDA Energy Assistance Program PY2027 eligibility, required-document, application, and appeal guidance.
Housing Choice Voucher: IHCDA 2026 HCV Administrative Plan; IHCDA HCV program materials; HUD HCV applicant guidance and income-limit framework.
School meals: Indiana Department of Education 2026–27 Income Eligibility Guidelines and Direct Certification guidance.
